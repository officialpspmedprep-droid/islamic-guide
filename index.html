Prophet Adam (AS) - The First Human and Prophet

Period: Approximately 4000 BC Location: Earth (Started in Paradise) Mentioned in Quran: 25 times People: First Humanity
Timeline: The first human being and first prophet of Allah
Main Message: Worship Allah alone, avoid Satan's deception, and seek forgiveness for mistakes.

Detailed Story

Prophet Adam (AS) holds the unique distinction of being the first human being created by Allah and the first prophet. His creation was a magnificent demonstration of Allah's power and wisdom.

Allah created Adam from clay, molding him with His own hands and breathing into him His spirit. The angels were commanded to prostrate to Adam out of respect for this new creation that contained Allah's spirit. All angels complied except Iblis (Satan), who refused out of arrogance, claiming he was superior as he was created from fire while Adam was created from clay.

Allah taught Adam the names of all things, giving him knowledge that even the angels didn't possess. When the angels couldn't name the things Allah showed them, Adam demonstrated his knowledge, proving why he was chosen as Allah's vicegerent on Earth.

Adam and his wife Hawwa (Eve) lived in Paradise, enjoying its blessings with only one prohibition: not to approach a particular tree. Satan, now eternally cursed for his disobedience, deceived them into eating from the forbidden tree. Immediately they realized their mistake and their nakedness became apparent to them.

فَأَكَلَا مِنْهَا فَبَدَتْ لَهُمَا سَوْءَٰتُهُمَا وَطَفِقَا يَخْصِفَانِ عَلَيْهِمَا مِن وَرَقِ ٱلْجَنَّةِ
"So they both ate of it, and their nakedness became apparent to them, and they began to fasten over themselves from the leaves of Paradise."
Surah Taha (20:121)

Despite their disobedience, Adam and Hawwa immediately turned to Allah in sincere repentance. Allah, in His infinite mercy, accepted their repentance but sent them down to Earth to live there and reproduce. This descent wasn't a punishment but part of Allah's divine plan for humanity to inhabit and develop the Earth.

On Earth, Adam received revelation from Allah and became the first prophet to guide his children. He taught them about Allah, worship, and how to live righteous lives. He had many children, and from them, the human race multiplied and spread across the Earth.

Miracles

Creation from Clay: Adam's very creation was miraculous - formed from clay and given life by Allah's spirit.
Knowledge of All Names: Allah taught Adam the names of all things, giving him knowledge beyond the angels.
Prostration of Angels: The command for angels to prostrate to Adam demonstrated his honored status.

Major Challenges

Satan's Deception: The constant enmity of Satan who tricked Adam and Hawwa into disobeying Allah's command.

Life on Earth: Adapting to life outside Paradise, dealing with hardship, and establishing human civilization.

Guiding Children: Teaching his numerous children about Tawheed (monotheism) and righteous living.

Key Duas (Supplications)

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْخَـٰسِرِينَ
"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."
Surah Al-A'raf (7:23)

This beautiful dua of Adam and Hawwa after their mistake demonstrates the proper way to seek forgiveness - acknowledging the wrongdoing, admitting it was their own fault, and completely relying on Allah's mercy.

Lessons for Humanity

Human Dignity: Every human being deserves respect as we all carry the spirit of Allah within us.
Importance of Repentance: No matter how grave the mistake, sincere repentance can always reach Allah.
Satan's Eternal Enmity: We must always be aware of Satan's tricks and never trust his whispers.
Knowledge Value: The pursuit of knowledge is highly valued in Islam, as demonstrated by Adam being taught the names.
Accountability: Every action has consequences, and we are accountable for our choices.

Prophet Idris (AS) - The Wise Scholar

Period: After Adam, Before Nuh Location: Mesopotamia Mentioned in Quran: 2 times Known For: Wisdom and Knowledge
Timeline: First to write with pen, known for wisdom and knowledge
Main Message: Pursue knowledge, practice wisdom, and maintain righteousness.

Detailed Story

Prophet Idris (AS) was a prophet known for his exceptional wisdom and knowledge. He lived after Prophet Adam and before Prophet Nuh. Idris was the first human to write with a pen and the first to study astronomy and mathematics.

Allah elevated Idris to a high status due to his devotion and knowledge. He taught people various skills and crafts that helped in building civilization. He emphasized the importance of seeking knowledge and using it for the benefit of humanity.

وَٱذْكُرْ فِى ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ إِدْرِيسَ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صِدِّيقًۭا نَّبِيًّۭا ﴿٥٦﴾ وَرَفَعْنَـٰهُ مَكَانًا عَلِيًّا
"And mention in the Book, Idrees. Indeed, he was a man of truth and a prophet. And We raised him to a high station."
Surah Maryam (19:56-57)

Idris was known for his strict adherence to truth and his continuous worship of Allah. He divided his time between teaching people practical skills and guiding them spiritually. He established the foundation of many sciences that would benefit future generations.

According to Islamic tradition, Idris was taken up to heaven without experiencing death, similar to Prophet Isa. He continues to be revered as a symbol of knowledge and wisdom in Islamic tradition.

Idris taught his people about astronomy, mathematics, and various crafts. He emphasized that all knowledge comes from Allah and should be used for righteous purposes. His life demonstrated the harmony between spiritual devotion and intellectual pursuit.

Miracles

Elevation to Heaven: Was raised to a high station by Allah without experiencing death.
Knowledge Revelation: Received divine knowledge in various sciences.
First Writer: First human to write with a pen, preserving knowledge for future generations.

Major Challenges

Establishing Civilization: Teaching primitive people advanced skills and knowledge.

Spiritual Guidance: Balancing material progress with spiritual development.

Knowledge Preservation: Developing systems to preserve and transmit knowledge.

Key Duas (Supplications)

"O Allah, increase me in knowledge and understanding."
Inspired by the example of Idris (AS)

While specific duas of Idris are not mentioned in the Quran, his life teaches us to seek knowledge and wisdom through supplication to Allah.

Lessons for Humanity

Value of Knowledge: The importance of seeking and applying knowledge for human development.
Wisdom in Action: Using knowledge wisely for the benefit of society and in accordance with Allah's will.
Balance: Maintaining balance between material and spiritual development.
Preservation of Knowledge: The importance of writing and preserving knowledge for future generations.
Divine Elevation: Those who seek knowledge and practice righteousness are elevated by Allah.

Prophet Nuh (AS) - The Persistent Warner

Period: Approximately 3000 BC Location: Mesopotamia Mentioned in Quran: 43 times Preached for 950 years
Timeline: Preached for 950 years, built the ark, saved believers from flood
Main Message: Worship Allah alone and abandon idol worship, or face destruction.

Detailed Story

Prophet Nuh (AS) was sent to a people who had fallen into idol worship. They worshipped statues called Wadd, Suwa, Yaghuth, Ya'uq, and Nasr, which were originally righteous people whose images were made into idols after their deaths.

Nuh preached to his people for 950 years, calling them to abandon idol worship and return to the worship of Allah alone. He warned them of severe punishment if they continued in their disbelief.

وَلَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِۦ فَلَبِثَ فِيهِمْ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ إِلَّا خَمْسِينَ عَامًۭا فَأَخَذَهُمُ ٱلطُّوفَانُ وَهُمْ ظَـٰلِمُونَ
"And We certainly sent Noah to his people, and he remained among them a thousand years minus fifty years, and the flood seized them while they were wrongdoers."
Surah Al-Ankabut (29:14)

Despite Nuh's continuous efforts, only a small number of poor and weak people believed in him. The wealthy and powerful leaders mocked him and accused him of being misguided. They demanded that he drive away the poor believers if he wanted them to listen to him.

After centuries of rejection, Nuh made a sincere prayer to Allah, asking that no unbeliever be left on Earth. Allah accepted his prayer and commanded him to build an ark. The people mocked him when he started building the ark far from any water source.

When the signs of flood appeared, Allah commanded Nuh to load the ark with pairs of every animal and the believers. Nuh's own son refused to board the ark, claiming he would climb a mountain to save himself. The flood waters rose and covered everything, including the mountains.

وَحَمَلْنَـٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلْوَٰحٍۢ وَدُسُرٍۢ ﴿١٣﴾ تَجْرِى بِأَعْيُنِنَا جَزَآءًۭ لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ
"And We carried him on a [construction of] planks and nails, sailing under Our observation as reward for he who had been denied."
Surah Al-Qamar (54:13-14)

After the flood subsided, the ark settled on Mount Judi, and the believers started a new civilization. All of humanity today are descendants of those who were saved in Nuh's ark.

Miracles

The Great Ark: Built a massive ark that saved believers and animals from the flood.
Longevity in Preaching: Preached for 950 years with unwavering patience.
Answered Prayer: His prayer for the destruction of disbelievers was accepted.

Major Challenges

950 Years of Rejection: Preaching for centuries with very few converts.

Mockery and Opposition: Faced continuous mockery from his people.

Family Tragedy: His own son rejected faith and drowned in the flood.

Building the Ark: Constructing a massive vessel without modern tools.

Key Duas (Supplications)

رَّبِّ ٱغْفِرْ لِى وَلِوَٰلِدَىَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيْتِىَ مُؤْمِنًۭا وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَـٰتِ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارًۢا
"My Lord, forgive me and my parents and whoever enters my house a believer and the believing men and believing women. And do not increase the wrongdoers except in destruction."
Surah Nuh (71:28)

Lessons for Humanity

Ultimate Patience: Nuh demonstrates the highest level of patience in da'wah.
Never Despair: Continue calling to Allah regardless of the response.
Family Test: Even prophets can face tests through their family members.
Divine Justice: Allah's punishment inevitably comes to those who reject truth.

Prophet Hud (AS) - Warner to the People of 'Ad

Period: After Nuh Location: Southern Arabia Mentioned in Quran: 7 times People: Tribe of 'Ad
Timeline: Sent to powerful people of 'Ad, warned against arrogance, destroyed by violent wind
Main Message: Avoid arrogance and worship Allah alone, or face destruction.

Detailed Story

Prophet Hud (AS) was sent to the people of 'Ad, who were descendants of Prophet Nuh's people. The 'Ad were a powerful and prosperous civilization known for their massive buildings and physical strength.

The people of 'Ad had become arrogant due to their power and wealth. They worshipped idols and oppressed others. Hud called them to abandon idol worship and return to the worship of Allah alone.

وَإِلَىٰ عَادٍ أَخَاهُمْ هُودًۭا ۚ قَالَ يَـٰقَوْمِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنْ إِلَـٰهٍ غَيْرُهُۥٓ ۖ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ
"And to 'Aad [We sent] their brother Hud. He said, 'O my people, worship Allah; you have no deity other than Him. Then will you not fear Him?'"
Surah Al-A'raf (7:65)

The people mocked Hud and challenged him to bring the punishment he threatened. They boasted about their strength and claimed nothing could destroy them. Despite Hud's continuous warnings, they persisted in their arrogance and disbelief.

After years of rejection, Allah sent upon them a devastating wind that destroyed everything for seven nights and eight days. The once-mighty people of 'Ad were completely annihilated, while Hud and his few followers were saved.

فَأَخَذَتْهُمُ ٱلصَّيْحَةُ بِٱلْحَقِّ فَجَعَلْنَـٰهُمْ غُثَآءًۭ ۚ فَبُعْدًۭا لِّلْقَوْمِ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ
"So the shriek seized them in truth, and We made them as [plant] stubble. So away with the wrongdoing people."
Surah Al-Muminun (23:41)

Miracles

Destruction of 'Ad: The complete annihilation of the powerful 'Ad civilization.
Protection of Believers: Hud and his followers were saved from destruction.

Major Challenges

Arrogant People: Dealing with people who were proud of their power and wealth.

Mockery and Rejection: Being mocked and rejected despite clear warnings.

Limited Response: Very few people accepted his message.

Lessons for Humanity

Danger of Arrogance: Arrogance leads to destruction regardless of power.
Wealth as Test: Material wealth is a test from Allah, not a sign of superiority.
Divine Justice: No civilization is too powerful to escape divine punishment.

Prophet Saleh (AS) - The Prophet of the She-Camel

Period: After Hud Location: Al-Hijr (Northern Arabia) Mentioned in Quran: 9 times People: Tribe of Thamud
Timeline: Sent to people of Thamud, miracle of she-camel, people destroyed by earthquake
Main Message: Worship Allah alone and respect His signs, or face destruction.

Detailed Story

Prophet Saleh (AS) was sent to the people of Thamud, who were descendants of the people of 'Ad. The Thamud were known for their skill in carving homes out of mountains and their advanced civilization.

Like their predecessors, the Thamud had abandoned the worship of Allah and were engaged in idol worship. Saleh called them to Tawheed and warned them of Allah's punishment if they persisted in disbelief.

The people demanded a miracle from Saleh as proof of his prophethood. Allah granted them a she-camel that emerged from a mountain as a sign. This camel was extraordinary and served as a test for the people.

وَءَاتَيْنَا ثَمُودَ ٱلنَّاقَةَ مُبْصِرَةًۭ فَظَلَمُوا۟ بِهَا
"And We gave the Thamud the she-camel as a visible sign, but they wronged her."
Surah Al-Isra (17:59)

Allah commanded that the camel should drink water on one day while the people's animals would drink on the next day. Despite this clear miracle, the people plotted to kill the camel. The most wicked among them hamstrung and killed the she-camel.

After they killed the camel, Saleh warned them that they had three days before destruction would come. On the fourth day, a mighty earthquake destroyed them, while Saleh and his followers were saved.

فَعَقَرُوا۟ ٱلنَّاقَةَ وَعَتَوْا۟ عَنْ أَمْرِ رَبِّهِمْ وَقَالُوا۟ يَـٰصَـٰلِحُ ٱئْتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ﴿٧٧﴾ فَأَخَذَتْهُمُ ٱلرَّجْفَةُ فَأَصْبَحُوا۟ فِى دَارِهِمْ جَـٰثِمِينَ
"So they hamstrung the she-camel and were insolent toward the command of their Lord and said, 'O Salih, bring us what you promise us, if you should be of the messengers.' So the earthquake seized them, and they became within their home [corpses] fallen prone."
Surah Al-A'raf (7:77-78)

Miracles

The She-Camel: A miraculous camel emerging from solid rock.
Destruction of Thamud: Complete annihilation of the Thamud civilization.

Major Challenges

Demand for Miracles: People demanding signs while already having clear evidence.

Killing the Miracle: The people killing the very sign given to them.

Arrogance: Dealing with people who were proud of their technological achievements.

Lessons for Humanity

Respect for Signs: The importance of respecting Allah's signs and miracles.
Technology and Faith: Advanced technology doesn't replace the need for faith.
Consequences of Defiance: Defying clear signs leads to severe punishment.

Prophet Ibrahim (AS) - The Friend of Allah

Period: Approximately 2000 BC Location: Mesopotamia, Palestine Mentioned in Quran: 69 times Title: Khalilullah (Friend of Allah)
Timeline: Born in Ur, destroyed idols, thrown in fire, built Kaaba with Ismail
Main Message: Pure monotheism (Tawheed) - worship Allah alone without partners.

Detailed Story

Prophet Ibrahim (AS) is one of the greatest prophets in Islam, known as the "Father of Prophets." He was born in Ur, Mesopotamia, during the reign of the tyrant King Nimrod.

From childhood, Ibrahim questioned the idol worship of his people. He used logic and reasoning to show them the falsehood of worshipping idols that could neither benefit nor harm anyone.

إِذْ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوْمِهِۦ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ ﴿٧٠﴾ قَالُوا۟ نَعْبُدُ أَصْنَامًۭا فَنَظَلُّ لَهَا عَـٰكِفِينَ ﴿٧١﴾ قَالَ هَلْ يَسْمَعُونَكُمْ إِذْ تَدْعُونَ ﴿٧٢﴾ أَوْ يَنفَعُونَكُمْ أَوْ يَضُرُّونَ ﴿٧٣﴾
"When he said to his father and his people, 'What do you worship?' They said, 'We worship idols and remain to them devoted.' He said, 'Do they hear you when you supplicate? Or do they benefit you, or do they harm?'"
Surah Ash-Shu'ara (26:70-73)

Ibrahim destroyed the idols of his people, leaving only the largest one intact. When the people confronted him, he told them to ask the big idol who had broken the others. They realized the foolishness of their worship but still refused to believe.

King Nimrod ordered Ibrahim to be thrown into a huge fire as punishment. But Allah commanded the fire to be cool and safe for Ibrahim, and he emerged unharmed.

قُلْنَا يَـٰنَارُ كُونِى بَرْدًۭا وَسَلَـٰمًا عَلَىٰٓ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ
"We said, 'O fire, be coolness and safety upon Abraham.'"
Surah Al-Anbiya (21:69)

Ibrahim migrated with his wife Sarah and nephew Lut to Palestine. When Sarah remained childless, she gave her Egyptian handmaid Hajar to Ibrahim, who bore him Ismail. Later, Allah blessed Ibrahim and Sarah with Ishaq in their old age.

Allah tested Ibrahim with the command to sacrifice his son Ismail. Both father and son demonstrated ultimate submission to Allah's will. At the last moment, Allah replaced Ismail with a ram, demonstrating that it was the willingness to sacrifice that mattered.

Ibrahim and Ismail built the Kaaba in Mecca as the first house of worship for Allah. Ibrahim established the rites of Hajj and prayed for a prophet to come from his descendants to guide humanity.

Miracles

Fire Protection: Emerged unharmed from a massive fire.
Resurrection of Birds: Allah showed him how He brings the dead to life.
Building Kaaba: Built the first house of worship with his son Ismail.
Father of Prophets: Many prophets came from his lineage.

Major Challenges

Idol Worship Opposition: Standing alone against his people's idolatry.

Being Thrown in Fire: Facing death by burning for his faith.

Sacrificing His Son: The ultimate test of submission to Allah.

Migration: Leaving his homeland multiple times for Allah's sake.

Key Duas (Supplications)

The most famous story of Yaqub is his profound love for his son Yusuf and the subsequent trials he faced when Yusuf was taken from him. Yusuf's brothers, jealous of their father's affection for him, plotted to get rid of him.

They threw Yusuf into a well and then brought his shirt stained with false blood to their father, claiming that a wolf had eaten him. Yaqub, being a prophet, knew they were lying but demonstrated incredible patience.

After many years, when Yusuf had become the ruler of Egypt, he arranged for his family to join him. Yaqub was reunited with his son Yusuf after decades of separation. When Yusuf's shirt was placed on Yaqub's face, his eyesight was restored by Allah's mercy.

Prophet Yusuf (AS) - The Dream Interpreter

Prophet Yusuf (AS) was the beloved son of Prophet Yaqub (AS). From childhood, he was known for his exceptional beauty and wisdom. He had a dream in which eleven stars, the sun, and the moon were prostrating to him.

إِذْ قَالَ يُوسُفُ لِأَبِيهِ يَـٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّى رَأَيْتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوْكَبًۭا وَٱلشَّمْسَ وَٱلْقَمَرَ رَأَيْتُهُمْ لِى سَـٰجِدِينَ

Yusuf was rescued from the well by a caravan and sold as a slave in Egypt to a nobleman named Al-Aziz. In Al-Aziz's house, Yusuf grew into a handsome young man. Al-Aziz's wife, Zulaikha, tried to seduce him, but Yusuf remained steadfast in his purity and fled from her advances.

وَرَٰوَدَتْهُ ٱلَّتِى هُوَ فِى بَيْتِهَا عَن نَّفْسِهِۦ وَغَلَّقَتِ ٱلْأَبْوَٰبَ وَقَالَتْ هَيْتَ لَكَ ۚ قَالَ مَعَاذَ ٱللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ رَبِّىٓ أَحْسَنَ مَثْوَاىَ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفْلِحُ ٱلظَّـٰلِمُونَ

Prophet Musa (AS) is one of the most frequently mentioned prophets in the Quran. His story begins during the reign of Pharaoh in Egypt, who had ordered the killing of all newborn Israelite boys.

When Musa was born, his mother received divine inspiration to put him in a basket and place it in the river. The basket floated to Pharaoh's palace, where Asiyah, Pharaoh's wife, found the baby and convinced Pharaoh to adopt him.

وَأَوْحَيْنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنْ أَرْضِعِيهِ ۖ فَإِذَا خِفْتِ عَلَيْهِ فَأَلْقِيهِ فِى ٱلْيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِى وَلَا تَحْزَنِىٓ ۖ إِنَّا رَآدُّوهُ إِلَيْكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ
"And We inspired to the mother of Moses, 'Suckle him; but when you fear for him, cast him into the river and do not fear and do not grieve. Indeed, We will return him to you and will make him [one] of the messengers.'"
Surah Al-Qasas (28:7)

Musa grew up in Pharaoh's palace but never forgot his Israelite roots. One day, he accidentally killed an Egyptian who was fighting with an Israelite. Fearing for his life, Musa fled to Madyan.

In Madyan, Musa helped two women water their flocks and was invited by their father (Prophet Shu'aib) to stay. He married one of the daughters and lived there for eight to ten years.

While traveling with his family, Musa saw a fire on Mount Tur. When he approached it, Allah spoke to him directly and appointed him as a prophet. Allah gave Musa two miracles: his staff turning into a snake and his hand becoming radiantly white.

Musa, accompanied by his brother Harun, confronted Pharaoh and showed him the miracles. Pharaoh's magicians challenged Musa, but when they saw his miracle, they immediately believed in Allah.

Despite numerous signs, Pharaoh and his people persisted in disbelief. Allah sent upon them various plagues, but they still didn't believe. Finally, Allah commanded Musa to lead the Israelites out of Egypt at night.

When Pharaoh pursued them with his army, Allah parted the Red Sea for the Israelites to cross safely. When Pharaoh and his army followed, the sea closed over them, drowning them all.

Miracles

Staff Transformation: His staff turned into a large snake.
Radiant Hand: His hand became brilliantly white.
Parting the Red Sea: The sea parted allowing Israelites to cross.
Water from Rock: Struck a rock and water gushed forth.
Receiving Torah: Spoke directly to Allah and received the Torah.

Major Challenges

Confronting Pharaoh: Facing the most powerful ruler of his time.

Stubborn People: Leading Israelites who frequently complained and rebelled.

40 Years in Wilderness: Wandering with a stubborn nation for decades.

Golden Calf Incident: His people worshipped a calf in his absence.

"My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need."
Surah Al-Qasas (28:24)

Lessons for Humanity

Prophet Harun (AS) - The Spokesman Prophet

Period: Approximately 1300 BC Location: Egypt, Sinai Peninsula Mentioned in Quran: 20 times Brother and Assistant to Musa
Timeline: Brother of Musa, assisted in confronting Pharaoh, high priest of Bani Israel
Main Message: Supporting truth, eloquent advocacy, and maintaining faith during trials.

Detailed Story

Prophet Harun (AS) was the elder brother of Prophet Musa (AS) and was appointed by Allah as a prophet to assist his brother in his mission. He was known for his eloquence and persuasive speaking abilities.

When Allah appointed Musa as a prophet and commanded him to go to Pharaoh, Musa expressed concern about his speech impediment. He asked Allah to appoint Harun as his assistant because of Harun's eloquence.

وَٱجْعَل لِّى وَزِيرًۭا مِّنْ أَهْلِى ﴿٢٩﴾ هَـٰرُونَ أَخِى ﴿٣٠﴾ ٱشْدُدْ بِهِۦٓ أَزْرِى ﴿٣١﴾ وَأَشْرِكْهُ فِىٓ أَمْرِى
"And appoint for me a minister from my family - Aaron, my brother. Increase through him my strength And let him share my task."
Surah Taha (20:29-32)

Allah granted Musa's request and appointed Harun as a prophet to assist him. Together, they confronted Pharaoh and delivered Allah's message. Harun's eloquence complemented Musa's leadership, making them an effective team.

When Musa went to Mount Sinai to receive the Torah, he left Harun in charge of the Israelites. During Musa's absence, a man named Samiri created a golden calf which the Israelites began to worship.

Harun tried to stop them from this great sin, but they refused to listen and even threatened to kill him. When Musa returned and saw what had happened, he was angry but understood Harun's difficult position.

Harun continued to serve as the high priest of the Israelites and supported Musa throughout their journey in the wilderness. He was known for his wisdom, patience, and dedication to spreading Allah's message.

Miracles

Prophethood: Appointed as a prophet by Allah to assist his brother.
Eloquence: Gifted with exceptional speaking abilities.
Support Role: Perfect complement to his brother's mission.

Major Challenges

Confronting Pharaoh: Facing the tyrannical ruler alongside his brother.

Golden Calf Incident: Dealing with rebellion during Musa's absence.

Supporting Role: Working in the shadow of his more prominent brother.

Lessons for Humanity

Supporting Role Importance: Value of being a supportive team member.
Brotherly Cooperation: Power of sibling cooperation in achieving goals.
Eloquent Advocacy: Using communication skills for spreading truth.

Prophet Dhul-Kifl (AS) - The Patient and Faithful

Period: Uncertain (After Musa) Location: Likely in the Levant region Mentioned in Quran: 2 times Known For: Patience and Fulfilling Promises
Timeline: Known for his faithfulness and patience, possibly a judge or ruler
Main Message: Faithfulness to promises and patience in fulfilling responsibilities.

Detailed Story

Prophet Dhul-Kifl (AS) is one of the lesser-known prophets mentioned in the Quran. The name "Dhul-Kifl" means "the one with a double portion" or "the guarantor," indicating his characteristic of fulfilling promises and taking responsibility.

There are different opinions among scholars about his identity. Some believe he was the son of Prophet Ayyub (AS), while others suggest he was a prophet sent to the Israelites after Prophet Ilyas (AS). Some identify him with the biblical prophet Ezekiel.

What is clear from the Quran is that Dhul-Kifl was known for his exceptional patience, faithfulness, and commitment to his responsibilities. He was among the righteous prophets whom Allah praised.

وَإِسْمَـٰعِيلَ وَإِدْرِيسَ وَذَا ٱلْكِفْلِ ۖ كُلٌّۭ مِّنَ ٱلصَّـٰبِرِينَ
"And [mention] Ishmael and Idrees and Dhul-Kifl; all were of the patient."
Surah Al-Anbiya (21:85)

According to various Islamic traditions, Dhul-Kifl was known for his justice and fairness in judging among people. He would spend his days judging between people with fairness and his nights in prayer and worship of Allah.

One famous story about Dhul-Kifl illustrates his commitment to his promises. He made a commitment to fast during the day, pray at night, and never get angry. Satan tried to test him by creating situations that would make him angry, but Dhul-Kifl remained patient and fulfilled his promises.

Despite the limited information about him in the Quran, his mention among other great prophets indicates his high status in Allah's sight. His life serves as an example of faithfulness, patience, and commitment to responsibilities.

Miracles

Exceptional Patience: His ability to remain patient in all circumstances.
Faithfulness: His unwavering commitment to promises and responsibilities.

Major Challenges

Maintaining Vows: The challenge of keeping difficult promises.

Satanic Tests: Facing attempts by Satan to make him break commitments.

Lessons for Humanity

Promise Keeping: Importance of fulfilling promises and commitments.
Patience as Worship: Patience is not just endurance but an act of worship.
Responsibility: Taking responsibility for commitments seriously.

Prophet Dawud (AS) - The King and Prophet

Period: Approximately 1000 BC Location: Palestine Mentioned in Quran: 16 times Received Zabur (Psalms)
Timeline: Defeated Goliath, became king of Israel, received Zabur, father of Sulaiman
Main Message: Justice, humility before Allah, and using power for righteousness.

Detailed Story

Prophet Dawud (AS) was both a king and a prophet of Allah. His story begins when he was a young shepherd boy serving in the army of King Talut (Saul).

The Israelites were facing the mighty army of Jalut (Goliath), a giant warrior who challenged any Israelite to single combat. Despite being young and inexperienced in warfare, Dawud accepted the challenge with faith in Allah.

فَهَزَمُوهُم بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ وَقَتَلَ دَاوُۥدُ جَالُوتَ وَءَاتَىٰهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْمُلْكَ وَٱلْحِكْمَةَ وَعَلَّمَهُۥ مِمَّا يَشَآءُ
"So they defeated them by permission of Allah, and David killed Goliath, and Allah gave him the kingship and wisdom and taught him from that which He willed."
Surah Al-Baqarah (2:251)

Dawud defeated Jalut with a simple slingshot and stones, demonstrating that victory comes from Allah, not from physical strength alone. After this victory, Allah granted him kingship and prophethood.

Allah gave Dawud many blessings and miracles. The mountains and birds would glorify Allah with him, iron became soft in his hands like clay, he could make armor and weapons from iron, he received the divine scripture Zabur (Psalms), and he was given a beautiful voice for praising Allah.

Dawud was known for his strict justice and his habit of dividing his time between ruling, worship, and family. He would spend one day for judging among people, one day for worship, one day for preaching, and one day for personal matters.

Dawud established a powerful kingdom and was succeeded by his son Sulaiman. He is remembered for his justice, his beautiful recitation of Zabur, and his constant devotion to Allah.

Miracles

Defeating Goliath: As a young boy, defeated the giant warrior.
Soft Iron: Iron became soft in his hands like clay.
Nature's Praise: Mountains and birds joined him in praising Allah.
Beautiful Voice: His recitation of Zabur was exceptionally beautiful.

Major Challenges

Facing Goliath: Confronting a giant warrior as a young shepherd.

Kingship Responsibilities: Balancing prophecy with political leadership.

Judging Fairly: Making just decisions as a ruler and judge.

Lessons for Humanity

Faith Over Power: True strength comes from faith in Allah.
Justice and Humility: Even kings must remain humble and just.
Art in Worship: Using talents in service of Allah.
Time Management: Balancing different responsibilities in life.

Prophet Sulaiman (AS) - The King of All Creation

Period: Approximately 950 BC Location: Palestine Mentioned in Quran: 17 times Son of Dawud
Timeline: Son of Dawud, kingdom over humans, jinn, animals and wind, built Jerusalem Temple
Main Message: All power belongs to Allah, and worldly possessions are tests from Him.

Detailed Story

Prophet Sulaiman (AS) was the son of Prophet Dawud (AS) and inherited both his father's kingdom and prophethood. Allah granted him a unique kingdom that no one before or after him would have.

From a young age, Sulaiman demonstrated exceptional wisdom. When he and his father were judging a case where someone's sheep had destroyed a crop at night, young Sulaiman suggested a fair solution that impressed everyone.

Allah granted Sulaiman extraordinary powers and dominions: control over the wind which would carry him wherever he wanted, authority over jinn who would work for him, understanding the language of animals and birds, control over devils and Shayateen, and molten brass that flowed for him like water.

وَلِسُلَيْمَـٰنَ ٱلرِّيحَ غُدُوُّهَا شَهْرٌۭ وَرَوَاحُهَا شَهْرٌۭ ۖ وَأَسَلْنَا لَهُۥ عَيْنَ ٱلْقِطْرِ
"And to Solomon [We subjected] the wind - its morning [journey was that of] a month - and its afternoon [journey was that of] a month, and We made flow for him a spring of [liquid] copper."
Surah Saba (34:12)

One famous story demonstrates Sulaiman's connection with nature. Once, while reviewing his army of humans, jinn, and birds, he noticed the Hoopoe bird was missing. When the Hoopoe returned, it brought news of the Queen of Sheba (Bilqis) and her people who worshipped the sun.

Sulaiman sent a letter to Bilqis inviting her to submit to Allah. When she came with gifts, Sulaiman demonstrated Allah's power by bringing her throne to his palace in the blink of an eye. When she entered his crystal palace, she thought it was water and lifted her dress, revealing her legs. She then realized the greatness of Sulaiman's kingdom and submitted to Allah.

Sulaiman built the magnificent Temple in Jerusalem and established a powerful kingdom. Despite his immense power, he remained humble and always acknowledged that everything came from Allah.

Miracles

Control Over Wind: Wind carried him wherever he wanted.
Authority Over Jinn: Jinn worked for him in construction.
Understanding Animal Language: Could communicate with birds and animals.
Instant Transportation: Brought Queen Bilqis's throne instantly.

Major Challenges

Managing Vast Kingdom: Ruling over humans, jinn, animals, and natural forces.

Maintaining Humility: Staying humble despite unprecedented power.

Testing with Wealth: The challenge of enormous wealth and power.

Lessons for Humanity

Power with Responsibility: Great power comes with responsibility to Allah.
Humility in Success: Importance of remaining humble despite achievements.
Wisdom in Leadership: Using intelligence in governance and judgment.
All Power Belongs to Allah: All abilities are granted by Allah, not personal achievements.

Prophet Ilyas (AS) - The Zealous Warner

Period: Approximately 900 BC Location: Baalbek (Lebanon) Mentioned in Quran: 3 times Known For: Challenging Baal Worship
Timeline: Sent to people of Baalbek who worshipped Baal, succeeded by Al-Yasa
Main Message: Abandon idol worship and return to worshipping Allah alone.

Detailed Story

Prophet Ilyas (AS) was sent to the people of Baalbek in Lebanon who had abandoned the worship of Allah and were worshipping an idol named Baal. This idol was considered the god of rain, fertility, and agriculture.

Ilyas called his people to abandon idol worship and return to Tawheed. He warned them of Allah's punishment if they continued in their shirk and corruption.

وَإِنَّ إِلْيَاسَ لَمِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ﴿١٢٣﴾ إِذْ قَالَ لِقَوْمِهِۦٓ أَلَا تَتَّقُونَ ﴿١٢٤﴾ أَتَدْعُونَ بَعْلًۭا وَتَذَرُونَ أَحْسَنَ ٱلْخَـٰلِقِينَ
"And indeed, Elias was from among the messengers, When he said to his people, 'Will you not fear Allah? Do you call upon Ba'l and leave the best of creators -'"
Surah As-Saffat (37:123-125)

The people rejected Ilyas and threatened him. To demonstrate the falsehood of their idol, Ilyas challenged the priests of Baal to a contest. He proposed that both sides prepare a sacrifice and call upon their deity to send fire from heaven. The true God would answer with fire.

The priests of Baal prepared their sacrifice and called upon their idol from morning until evening, but no response came. Then Ilyas prepared his sacrifice and called upon Allah. Immediately, fire descended from heaven and consumed Ilyas's sacrifice.

Despite this clear miracle, most people still didn't believe. Only a small number accepted faith. Ilyas continued preaching for many years, facing persecution and rejection.

When the people persisted in their disbelief, Allah sent a drought upon them for three years as punishment. The people suffered greatly but still didn't repent. Finally, Ilyas prayed for rain, and Allah sent rain to relieve the believers.

Ilyas was eventually succeeded by Prophet Al-Yasa (AS). According to Islamic tradition, Ilyas did not die but was raised to heaven alive, and he returns to earth periodically.

Miracles

Fire from Heaven: Called down fire from heaven to consume his sacrifice.
Control Over Rain: Prayed for drought and later for rain.
Elevation to Heaven: Raised to heaven without experiencing death.

Major Challenges

Idol Worship Opposition: Challenging deeply entrenched idol worship.

Priestly Opposition: Facing strong opposition from priests of Baal.

Persecution: Being threatened and persecuted for his message.

Lessons for Humanity

Challenging Falsehood: Importance of openly challenging shirk.
Divine Evidence: Using demonstrations to prove truth.
Persistence: Continuing da'wah despite opposition.

Prophet Al-Yasa (AS) - The Successor Prophet

Period: Approximately 800 BC Location: Palestine/Syria region Mentioned in Quran: 2 times Successor to Ilyas
Timeline: Student and successor of Ilyas, continued his mission, performed miracles
Main Message: Continuation of Ilyas's message against idol worship and calling to Tawheed.

Detailed Story

Prophet Al-Yasa (AS) was the student and successor of Prophet Ilyas (AS). He continued the mission of his teacher, calling the people to abandon idol worship and return to the worship of Allah alone.

Al-Yasa was chosen by Ilyas as his successor and was trained in prophethood. When Ilyas was raised to heaven, Al-Yasa took over the responsibility of guiding the people.

وَإِسْمَـٰعِيلَ وَٱلْيَسَعَ وَذَا ٱلْكِفْلِ ۖ وَكُلٌّۭ مِّنَ ٱلْأَخْيَارِ
"And [mention] Ishmael and Elisha and Dhul-Kifl, and all are among the outstanding."
Surah Sad (38:48)

Al-Yasa continued to face the same challenges as his predecessor - the people's stubbornness in worshipping the idol Baal and their rejection of Tawheed. He performed miracles to demonstrate the truth of his message, but most people remained in disbelief.

Among the miracles attributed to Al-Yasa are healing the sick and curing leprosy, raising a dead boy to life, multiplying food during times of scarcity, and parting the Jordan River to cross it.

Despite the miracles, only a small group of people followed him. He spent his life patiently calling people to Allah and warning them of the consequences of shirk.

Al-Yasa's mission was particularly challenging because he came after Ilyas, who had already shown clear miracles. The people's continued rejection demonstrated their deep-rooted attachment to idol worship.

He lived a long life dedicated to spreading the message of Tawheed and left behind a legacy of patience and perseverance in the face of continuous rejection.

Miracles

Healing Miracles: Cured the sick and those with leprosy.
Raising the Dead: Brought a dead boy back to life.
Food Multiplication: Multiplied food to feed people during famine.
Parting River: Parted the Jordan River to cross it.

Major Challenges

Following a Great Prophet: Challenge of succeeding a prophet like Ilyas.

Persistent Idol Worship: People's deep attachment to Baal worship.

Limited Response: Very few people accepted his message despite miracles.

Lessons for Humanity

Continuity of Mission: Importance of continuing righteous work.
Patience in Da'wah: Continuing to call to truth even when response is limited.
Student-Teacher Relationship: Value of learning from righteous scholars.

Prophet Yunus (AS) - The Prophet in the Whale

Period: Approximately 700 BC Location: Nineveh (Iraq) Mentioned in Quran: 6 times Surah Named After Him
Timeline: Sent to people of Nineveh, left in frustration, swallowed by whale, people accepted message
Main Message: Never despair of Allah's mercy, and always fulfill your prophetic duties.

Detailed Story

Prophet Yunus (AS) was sent to the people of Nineveh in Iraq, who were engaged in idol worship and sinful practices. He called them to worship Allah alone and abandon their evil ways.

For many years, Yunus preached to his people, but they stubbornly rejected his message. Frustrated by their continuous rejection, Yunus left the city without waiting for Allah's permission, warning them that punishment would come in three days.

وَذَا ٱلنُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَـٰضِبًۭا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِى ٱلظُّلُمَـٰتِ أَن لَّآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبْحَـٰنَكَ إِنِّى كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ
"And [mention] the man of the fish, when he went off in anger and thought that We would not decree [anything] upon him. And he called out within the darknesses, 'There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.'"
Surah Al-Anbiya (21:87)

After Yunus left, the people saw signs of impending punishment and collectively repented. They accepted Yunus's message and turned to Allah in sincere repentance. Allah accepted their repentance and lifted the punishment.

Meanwhile, Yunus boarded a ship that became overloaded. To lighten the load, lots were cast, and Yunus was chosen to be thrown overboard. A large whale swallowed him, and he found himself in three layers of darkness: the darkness of the whale's stomach, the darkness of the sea, and the darkness of the night.

In this desperate situation, Yunus realized his mistake and called out to Allah with the famous prayer that has become known as the prayer of those in distress. Allah accepted his repentance and commanded the whale to cast him out onto the shore.

When Yunus recovered, he returned to Nineveh and was amazed to find that his people had accepted Islam. They had turned to Allah in sincere repentance, and Allah had forgiven them.

Miracles

Survival in Whale: Remained alive inside the whale's stomach.
Answered Prayer: His desperate prayer was immediately answered.
Mass Repentance: His entire people repented and accepted Islam.

Major Challenges

People's Rejection: Facing continuous rejection from his people.

Impatience: Leaving his mission without Allah's permission.

Swallowed by Whale: Terrifying experience of being swallowed by whale.

Lessons for Humanity

Patience in Da'wah: Importance of patience in calling to Allah.
Allah's Infinite Mercy: No matter the sin, Allah's mercy is greater.
Sincere Repentance: Power of sincere repentance to change destinies.
Never Despair: Never lose hope in Allah's mercy and forgiveness.

Prophet Zakariya (AS) - The Guardian of Maryam

Period: Approximately 1st Century BC Location: Jerusalem Mentioned in Quran: 7 times Father of Yahya
Timeline: Priest in Jerusalem Temple, guardian of Maryam, prayed for son in old age, father of Yahya
Main Message: The power of sincere prayer and Allah's ability to grant children to the elderly.

Detailed Story

Prophet Zakariya (AS) was a righteous prophet and priest who served in the Temple in Jerusalem. He was known for his devotion to worship and his care for the affairs of the children of Israel.

Zakariya was married to a righteous woman named Isha (Elizabeth), but they had no children and were both advanced in age. Despite this, Zakariya continued to serve Allah faithfully.

He was appointed as the guardian of Maryam (Mary), the mother of Isa (Jesus). Maryam lived in the Temple and devoted herself to worship. Zakariya would find that she always had provisions that he hadn't provided for her.

كُلَّمَا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهَا زَكَرِيَّا ٱلْمِحْرَابَ وَجَدَ عِندَهَا رِزْقًۭا ۖ قَالَ يَـٰمَرْيَمُ أَنَّىٰ لَكِ هَـٰذَا ۖ قَالَتْ هُوَ مِنْ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرْزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ

Seeing Allah's blessings upon Maryam, Zakariya was inspired to pray for a child of his own, despite his old age and his wife's barrenness. He made a sincere and humble prayer to Allah.

رَبِّ هَبْ لِى مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةًۭ طَيِّبَةً ۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ
"My Lord, grant me from Yourself a good offspring. Indeed, You are the Hearer of supplication."
Surah Al-Imran (3:38)

While he was standing in prayer in the sanctuary, angels gave him the good news of a son named Yahya (John), who would be righteous and a prophet.

يَـٰزَكَرِيَّآ إِنَّا نُبَشِّرُكَ بِغُلَـٰمٍ ٱسْمُهُۥ يَحْيَىٰ لَمْ نَجْعَل لَّهُۥ مِن قَبْلُ سَمِيًّۭا
"O Zechariah, indeed We give you good tidings of a boy whose name will be John. We have not assigned to any before [this] name."
Surah Maryam (19:7)

Zakariya was amazed and asked how this could be possible given his and his wife's old age. The angel replied that this was easy for Allah, just as He had created him from nothing.

As a sign of this miracle, Zakariya was unable to speak for three days, communicating only through gestures. When his son Yahya was born, Zakariya's speech was restored, and he praised Allah.

Zakariya continued to serve as a prophet until he was martyred by the evil rulers of his time. His son Yahya carried on his mission of calling people to Allah.

Miracles

Son in Old Age: Granted a righteous son despite advanced age and wife's barrenness.
Answered Prayer: His sincere prayer for a child was miraculously answered.
Temporary Muteness: Lost his speech as a sign of the miracle.

Major Challenges

Childlessness: The pain of being childless until old age.

Old Age: The challenge of having children at an advanced age.

Martyrdom: Ultimately martyred for his faith by evil rulers.

Key Duas (Supplications)

Prophet Yahya (AS) - The Pure and Righteous

Period: Approximately 1st Century BC Location: Palestine Mentioned in Quran: 5 times Cousin of Isa (AS)
Timeline: Son of Zakariya, born miraculously to aged parents, known for piety, martyred for truth
Main Message: Piety, purity, and standing for truth regardless of consequences.

Detailed Story

Prophet Yahya (AS), known as John the Baptist in Christian tradition, was the son of Prophet Zakariya (AS) and was born miraculously to elderly parents. His birth was an answer to his father's sincere prayers.

From childhood, Yahya was granted wisdom and understanding by Allah. He was known for his exceptional piety, righteousness, and devotion to worship.

يَـٰيَحْيَىٰ خُذِ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ بِقُوَّةٍۢ ۖ وَءَاتَيْنَـٰهُ ٱلْحُكْمَ صَبِيًّۭا ﴿١٢﴾ وَحَنَانًۭا مِّن لَّدُنَّا وَزَكَوٰةًۭ ۖ وَكَانَ تَقِيًّۭا
"O John, take the Scripture with determination.' And We gave him judgement [while yet] a boy And affection from Us and purity, and he was fearing of Allah."
Surah Maryam (19:12-13)

Yahya lived a simple and ascetic life, wearing clothes made of hair and eating simple food. He spent his time in worship and calling people to righteousness.

He was a contemporary and relative of Prophet Isa (AS) and supported his mission. Yahya preached to the children of Israel, calling them to repent and return to the true teachings of the Torah.

Yahya was known for his courage in speaking truth to power. He openly criticized the ruler Herod for his immoral marriage to his brother's wife, Herodias. This courageous stand against corruption and immorality eventually led to his martyrdom.

According to tradition, Yahya was beheaded after Herodias's daughter danced for Herod and requested Yahya's head as a reward. Thus, Yahya became a martyr for speaking the truth against tyranny and immorality.

The Quran praises Yahya for his exceptional character and grants him peace in this world and the next.

Miracles

Miraculous Birth: Born to elderly parents who were previously childless.
Wisdom in Childhood: Granted wisdom and prophethood from a young age.
Exceptional Piety: Known for his extraordinary devotion and purity.

Major Challenges

Speaking Truth to Power: Confronting the tyrannical ruler Herod.

Martyrdom: Being executed for standing up for truth and morality.

Ascetic Life: Living a life of extreme simplicity and self-denial.

Lessons for Humanity

Courage in Truth: Importance of speaking truth even when facing powerful opponents.
Piety and Simplicity: Value of a simple, devout life focused on worship.
Moral Courage: Standing against immorality regardless of consequences.

Prophet Isa (AS) - The Messiah and Word of Allah

Period: Approximately 1st Century AD Location: Palestine Mentioned in Quran: 25 times Received Injil (Gospel)
Timeline: Born miraculously without father, performed miracles, raised to heaven, will return before Day of Judgment
Main Message: Worship Allah alone, follow the true teachings, and prepare for the Hereafter.

Detailed Story

Prophet Isa (AS) holds a special place in Islam as one of the greatest prophets and the Messiah. His birth was miraculous - he was born to the virgin Maryam without a father by the command of Allah.

When Maryam withdrew from her family to a eastern place, the angel Jibril appeared to her in the form of a man and gave her the news of a pure son.

إِنَّمَا ٱلْمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ٱبْنُ مَرْيَمَ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ وَكَلِمَتُهُۥٓ أَلْقَىٰهَآ إِلَىٰ مَرْيَمَ وَرُوحٌۭ مِّنْهُ
"The Messiah, Jesus, the son of Mary, was but a messenger of Allah and His word which He directed to Mary and a soul [created at a command] from Him."
Surah An-Nisa (4:171)

When Maryam returned to her people carrying Isa, they accused her of immorality. But the infant Isa spoke from the cradle, defending his mother and declaring his prophethood.

قَالَ إِنِّى عَبْدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِىَ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِى نَبِيًّۭا ﴿٣٠﴾ وَجَعَلَنِى مُبَارَكًا أَيْنَ مَا كُنتُ وَأَوْصَـٰنِى بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱلزَّكَوٰةِ مَا دُمْتُ حَيًّۭا
"He said, 'Indeed, I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Scripture and made me a prophet. And He has made me blessed wherever I am and has enjoined upon me prayer and zakah as long as I remain alive.'"
Surah Maryam (19:30-31)

Isa grew up and began his mission, calling the children of Israel back to the true worship of Allah. Allah supported him with numerous miracles: creating birds from clay and breathing life into them, healing the blind and lepers, raising the dead by Allah's permission, knowing what people had eaten and stored in their homes, and receiving the revelation of the Injil (Gospel).

وَرَسُولًا إِلَىٰ بَنِىٓ إِسْرَٰٓءِيلَ أَنِّى قَدْ جِئْتُكُم بِـَٔايَةٍۢ مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ ۖ أَنِّىٓ أَخْلُقُ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلطِّينِ كَهَيْـَٔةِ ٱلطَّيْرِ فَأَنفُخُ فِيهِ فَيَكُونُ طَيْرًۢا بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ
"And [make him] a messenger to the Children of Israel, [who will say], 'Indeed I have come to you with a sign from your Lord in that I design for you from clay [that which is] like the form of a bird, then I breathe into it and it becomes a bird by permission of Allah.'"
Surah Al-Imran (3:49)

Despite these clear miracles, most Jews rejected Isa and plotted against him. They wanted to crucify him, but Allah saved him and raised him to heaven. The person who was crucified was made to resemble Isa.

وَمَا قَتَلُوهُ وَمَا صَلَبُوهُ وَلَـٰكِن شُبِّهَ لَهُمْ
"And they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him; but [another] was made to resemble him to them."
Surah An-Nisa (4:157)

In Islamic belief, Isa will return to earth before the Day of Judgment. He will descend in Damascus, break the cross, kill the Dajjal (Antichrist), establish justice, and rule according to Islamic law. Then he will die a natural death and be buried next to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) in Medina.

Isa is highly respected in Islam as a great prophet, but Muslims reject the Christian belief in his divinity. He was a human prophet and servant of Allah.

Miracles

Virgin Birth: Born without a father by Allah's command.
Speaking from Cradle: Spoke as an infant to defend his mother.
Creating Birds: Created birds from clay and gave them life.
Healing Miracles: Cured the blind and lepers.
Raising the Dead: Brought dead people back to life.
Knowledge of Hidden Things: Knew what people had eaten and stored.

Major Challenges

Rejection by Jews: Most Jews rejected his message despite clear miracles.

Accusations Against Mother: Defending his mother against false accusations.

Plots to Kill Him: Facing continuous plots by Jewish leaders to kill him.

Misunderstanding of His Nature: People attributing divinity to him.

Lessons for Humanity

Allah's Absolute Power: Allah can create without any natural means.
Purity of Maryam: The importance of chastity and purity.
True Nature of Isa: He was a human prophet, not divine.
Allah's Protection: Allah protects His chosen servants from harm.

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) - The Final Messenger of Allah

Period: 571-632 CE Location: Mecca and Medina Mentioned in Quran: 4 times by name Title: Khatam an-Nabiyyin (Seal of the Prophets)
Timeline: Born in Mecca, received revelation at 40, migrated to Medina, established Islamic state, conquered Mecca, farewell pilgrimage
Main Message: Worship Allah alone without partners, follow the Quran and Sunnah, and Muhammad is the final messenger sent to all humanity.

Detailed Story

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is the final messenger of Allah, sent as a mercy to all worlds. His life represents the perfect example for all Muslims to follow in every aspect of life.

وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَـٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ
"And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds."
Surah Al-Anbiya (21:107)

Early Life (571-610 CE)

Muhammad (PBUH) was born in Mecca in the Year of the Elephant (571 CE). He belonged to the noble tribe of Quraysh, from the clan of Banu Hashim. His father Abdullah died before his birth, and his mother Amina died when he was six years old. He was raised by his grandfather Abdul-Muttalib and later by his uncle Abu Talib.

From childhood, he was known as "Al-Amin" (the Trustworthy) and "As-Sadiq" (the Truthful) for his impeccable character and honesty. He worked as a merchant and earned a reputation for his integrity. At age 25, he married Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, a wealthy and noble businesswoman who was 15 years his senior.

The First Revelation (610 CE)

At age 40, while meditating in Cave Hira during the month of Ramadan, Muhammad (PBUH) received the first revelation from Allah through Angel Jibril (Gabriel). The first words revealed were:

ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ﴿١﴾ خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍۢ ﴿٢﴾ ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ ﴿٣﴾ ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ﴿٤﴾ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ
"Recite in the name of your Lord who created - Created man from a clinging substance. Recite, and your Lord is the most Generous - Who taught by the pen - Taught man that which he knew not."
Surah Al-Alaq (96:1-5)

Meccan Period (610-622 CE)

For the first three years, Muhammad (PBUH) preached Islam secretly. Then Allah commanded him to deliver the message openly. He faced severe persecution from the Quraysh tribe, who opposed his message of monotheism that threatened their polytheistic traditions and economic interests.

Key events during this period:

  • Public Declaration: Called his people to Islam from Mount Safa
  • Persecution: Early Muslims were tortured, boycotted, and some were martyred
  • Year of Sorrow (619 CE): Lost his wife Khadijah and uncle Abu Talib in the same year
  • Night Journey (Isra and Miraj): Traveled from Mecca to Jerusalem and ascended to heavens in one night

Migration to Medina (622 CE)

When persecution became unbearable, Allah commanded Muhammad (PBUH) to migrate to Yathrib (later named Medina). This event, known as the Hijrah, marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar.

إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدْ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذْ أَخْرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ ثَانِىَ ٱثْنَيْنِ إِذْ هُمَا فِى ٱلْغَارِ إِذْ يَقُولُ لِصَـٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَا
"If you do not aid him [Muhammad] - Allah has already aided him when those who disbelieved had driven him out [of Makkah] as one of two, when they were in the cave and he said to his companion, 'Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us.'"
Surah At-Tawbah (9:40)

Medina Period (622-632 CE)

In Medina, Muhammad (PBUH) established the first Islamic state based on justice, equality, and divine guidance. Key achievements:

  • Constitution of Medina: Established a social contract between Muslims, Jews, and other tribes
  • Building the First Mosque: Constructed Masjid an-Nabawi as the community center
  • Defensive Battles: Led Muslims in defensive battles against Meccan aggression
  • Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (628 CE): Signed a peace treaty with Quraysh
  • Conquest of Mecca (630 CE): Returned to Mecca peacefully and forgave his former persecutors
  • Farewell Pilgrimage (632 CE): Delivered his final sermon establishing fundamental human rights

Final Days and Legacy

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) passed away on 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH (June 8, 632 CE) in Medina. He left behind the complete and final revelation - the Holy Quran - and his Sunnah (traditions) as guidance for all humanity until the Day of Judgment.

مَّا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَآ أَحَدٍۢ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمْ وَلَـٰكِن رَّسُولَ ٱللَّهِ وَخَاتَمَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ ۗ وَكَانَ ٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍ عَلِيمًۭا
"Muhammad is not the father of [any] one of your men, but [he is] the Messenger of Allah and last of the prophets. And ever is Allah, of all things, Knowing."
Surah Al-Ahzab (33:40)

Tahajjud Prayer - The Night Vigil

Note: Tahajjud is a special voluntary prayer performed after sleeping at night. It is one of the most virtuous prayers after the five obligatory ones.

1

Time of Tahajjud

Tahajjud is performed after waking up from sleep during the night, preferably in the last third of the night.

"Our Lord, the Blessed, the Superior, comes every night down on the nearest Heaven to us when the last third of the night remains, saying: 'Is there anyone to invoke Me, so that I may respond to his invocation? Is there anyone to ask Me, so that I may grant him his request? Is there anyone seeking My forgiveness, so that I may forgive him?'"
Sahih al-Bukhari
2

How to Perform

Tahajjud can be performed in units of 2 rakats each. There's no fixed number - you can pray 2, 4, 6, 8 or more rakats.

"The night prayer is offered as two rak'at followed by two rak'at and so on, and if you fear the approaching dawn, pray one rak'ah as witr."
Sahih al-Bukhari
3

Recommended Practices

  • Wake up for Tahajjud with the intention of seeking closeness to Allah
  • Begin with 2 light rakats before starting the main Tahajjud prayer
  • Recite longer portions of Quran during Tahajjud
  • Make abundant dua and seek forgiveness
  • End with Witr prayer
Spiritual Benefits

Tahajjud brings you closer to Allah, increases faith, and purifies the heart.

Special Rewards

Allah grants special honors and answers prayers during Tahajjud time.

"The closest that the Lord is to His slave is in the last part of the night, so if you can be among those who remember Allah at that time, then do so."
At-Tirmidhi
Protection

Tahajjud prayer protects from evil and brings barakah (blessings) in one's life.

"You should pray Qiyam al-Layl, for it is the habit of the righteous who came before you and it brings you closer to your Lord, expiates sins, prevents misdeeds, and protects from disease."
At-Tirmidhi

Important: The best time for Tahajjud is the last third of the night. If you cannot wake up at that time, any time after Isha prayer and before Fajr is acceptable.

Complete Salah Method Step by Step

Note: This is a detailed step-by-step guide to performing Salah according to the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

1

Purification (Taharah)

Perform Wudu (ablution) or Ghusl (full bath) if required. Ensure clothes and prayer area are clean.

الطَّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ
"Purification is half of faith."
Sahih Muslim
2

Facing Qibla

Stand facing the direction of Kaaba in Mecca. This direction is called Qibla.

فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ ٱلْمَسْجِدِ ٱلْحَرَامِ ۚ وَحَيْثُ مَا كُنتُمْ فَوَلُّوا۟ وُجُوهَكُمْ شَطْرَهُۥ
"So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Haram. And wherever you [believers] are, turn your faces toward it [in prayer]."
Surah Al-Baqarah (2:144)
3

Make Intention (Niyyah)

Make intention in your heart for which prayer you are performing (Fajr, Dhuhr, etc.).

"Actions are judged by intentions, and every person will have what they intended."
Sahih al-Bukhari
4

Takbiratul Ihram

Raise both hands to ears and say: "Allahu Akbar" (Allah is the Greatest)

اللهُ أَكْبَرُ
"Allah is the Greatest"
5

Standing (Qiyam)

Place right hand over left hand on chest. Recite opening supplication, then Surah Al-Fatihah followed by another Surah.

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلاَ إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ
"Glory and praise be to You, O Allah. Blessed is Your Name, Exalted is Your Majesty. There is no god but You."
6

Bowing (Ruku)

Say "Allahu Akbar" and bow down, placing hands on knees. Keep back straight and say:

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
"Glory to my Lord, the Most Great" (3 times)
7

Standing After Ruku

Rise up from bowing position saying:

سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ
"Allah hears those who praise Him"

Then while standing say:

رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ
"Our Lord, to You is all praise"
8

Prostration (Sujood)

Say "Allahu Akbar" and go into prostration. Place forehead, nose, both hands, knees, and toes on ground. Say:

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
"Glory to my Lord, the Most High" (3 times)
9

Sitting Between Prostrations

Sit up from prostration saying "Allahu Akbar". Sit briefly then perform second prostration.

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي، رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
"My Lord, forgive me. My Lord, forgive me."
10

Final Sitting (Tashahhud)

After completing all rakats, sit for Tashahhud and send blessings upon the Prophet.

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
"All greetings, prayers and good things are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous slaves of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His slave and Messenger."
11

Salam (Ending Prayer)

Turn face to right and say:

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ
"Peace and mercy of Allah be upon you"

Then turn to left and repeat the same.
